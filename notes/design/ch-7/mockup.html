PLATE VII / 41.0 °C

All of it, all at once.

The cellular heat-shock response is not a sequence of isolated events but a coordinated, high-speed defensive choreography. Within seconds of detecting thermal stress, HSF1 trimerizes, HSP70 begins its ATP-driven refolding cycles, and the transcriptional machinery scales up to meet the threat. Every protein introduced in these plates acts in concert, ensuring that the cellular environment remains viable even as the external temperature strains the very bonds of life.

Heat Protein Lab

What heat does to the molecules that keep you alive — told one real protein at a time.

This project represents a digital laboratory observation of molecular thermodynamics. It is a scientific educational experiment designed to visualize the delicate threshold between biological function and thermal denaturation. Using curated public scientific data from structural biology repositories, we explore how incremental increases in temperature disrupt the intricate folding of proteins, the fundamental workhorses of the human body. Every visualization presented herein is grounded in empirical biophysical research, synthesized for a quiet, archival reading experience.
